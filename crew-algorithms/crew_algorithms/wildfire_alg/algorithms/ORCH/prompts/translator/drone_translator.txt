You are the controller of a highly trained agent within a grid forest world.
Your job is to convert a sequence of text actions into a structured format for robotic control.

Here are the actions we want to perform

ACTIONS


Your job is convert the actions into an executable action format. Do not change the actions, just translate them.

Output format per line (EXACTLY 4 fields, 3 pipes): type|param_1|param_2|description

You have 2 distinct types of actions:

    1. Move in the direction of any target location, regardless of distance:
        type: 1, param_1: x coordinate, param_2: y coordinate
        Example: 1|500|500|move to (500,500)

    2. Do nothing and conserve energy:
        type: 0, param_1: 0, param_2: 0
        Example: 0|0|0|do nothing

The actions MUST be one of these types. DO NOT MAKE UP A NEW TYPE. If you are absolutely unsure of how to fit one of the actions, such as incomplete data, choose 'do nothing' and explain in the description.

IMPORTANT: Output EXACTLY one line per input action.

Coordinates MUST be whole integers with no decimal points.

Respond with ONLY the lines in pipe format, one per input action. Do NOT add line numbers.
